Workflow

A Structured Pipeline

How every assignment progresses from first brief to verified delivery.

01

Discuss

We review your reference images, target dimensions, intended usage, and file specifications.

02

Plan

We establish scale constraints, polygon targets, split tolerances, and assembly plans before modeling.

03

Create

Base proportions are established, followed by geometry development, clean topology passes, and surface detailing.

04

Review

Progress views are shared for feedback to ensure accuracy to your provided references.

05

Deliver

Final production assets are packaged and delivered in your requested formats (OBJ, STL, FBX, or renders).
